### Hi, I'm Andrea 👋

I am currently a Research Analyst at the Global Poverty Research Lab, where I focus on large-scale evidence synthesis and meta-analyses of anti-poverty programs (cash transfers, and business training interventions). I am firmly committed to rigorous empirical research and the advancement of evidence-based policymaking. My current position at Northwestern is solidifying my research toolkit and providing the analytical rigor I intend to bring to a doctoral program in Economics.

My academic path began with training in economics (VWL) at the University of St. Gallen, in Switzerland, where I am from, and continued with a specialization in microeconometrics and public policy at the Paris School of Economics. Complementing my academic training, I have gained RA and policy experience within the Swiss government (SECO–Cooperation) and at J-PAL in Morocco, where I contributed to the impact evaluation of a large-scale educational intervention.
